# Design

I redesigned the site around a modern Ivy-prep aesthetic inspired by Yale and classic East Coast collegiate style.

- **Colors:** Used Yale blue, navy, warm cream, and off-white to make the store feel premium, recognizable, and connected to Yale.
- **Typography:** Combined editorial serif headings with clean sans-serif text to create a classic collegiate feel while keeping the site easy to read.
- **Hierarchy:** Added stronger headlines, whitespace, dividers, and clear CTAs so customers can quickly understand where to look and what to do next.
- **Product presentation:** Made product images larger and cleaner, with consistent cards and an editorial product-detail layout so the merchandise gets more attention and is easier to browse.
- **Navigation:** Simplified and polished the header so customers can move between shopping, account, and other pages quickly.
- **Chat:** Styled the chatbot as a branded “Campus Concierge” so it feels like part of the shopping experience rather than a separate AI tool.
- **Motion:** Added subtle hover, page, product, and chat animations to make the site feel responsive and polished without distracting from shopping.
- **Responsive design:** Improved mobile layouts so products, navigation, forms, and chat remain easy to use on smaller screens.

Overall, these changes make Campus Customs feel more like a real premium Yale storefront, make products easier to discover, and create a more enjoyable and trustworthy shopping experience that should encourage customers to browse longer and buy.
