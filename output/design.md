# Design

Campus Customs now uses a modern American-heritage direction inspired by Buck Mason's quiet utility: warm neutrals, clean product photography, compact utility navigation, and editorial spacing. The design borrows the visual language of understated heritage retail without copying Buck Mason's text, identity, or assets.

- **Color:** White, warm stone, charcoal, muted green-black, and a restrained rust accent give the site a calm, premium foundation while keeping Campus Customs content and Yale context recognizable.
- **Typography:** A strong condensed sans-serif treatment for headlines, labels, prices, filters, and navigation gives the storefront a direct, contemporary retail voice and improves scanning.
- **Hierarchy:** Large uppercase campaign headlines, compact utility labels, thin rules, and generous whitespace make the next action clear without surrounding every element with a card.
- **Product presentation:** Consistent image ratios, neutral image backgrounds, subtle hover scale, and minimal product metadata make catalogue photography the strongest visual element and reduce browsing friction.
- **Navigation and account:** The sticky, compact header keeps Shop, Account, and the local shopping bag visible; the authenticated Account page shows the signed-in profile without exposing passwords.
- **Product detail and bag:** Plain rectangular size selectors, clear stock states, and a side shopping-bag drawer keep purchase decisions close to the product while preserving the existing database-backed behavior.
- **Chat:** Campus Concierge uses the same charcoal, cream, and rust system so recommendations and conversation feel like part of the store rather than a separate tool.
- **Motion:** Short fades, image zooms, drawer transitions, and hover states add feedback without distracting from products; reduced-motion preferences remain supported.
- **Responsive design:** The compact mobile menu, two-column catalogue, stacked product detail, usable filters, bag drawer, account dashboard, and chat panel adapt without horizontal overflow.

Together these choices make products easier to compare, the brand feel more trustworthy, and the shopping path more focused while retaining the real catalogue, inventory, authentication, cart, memory, and agent functionality.
